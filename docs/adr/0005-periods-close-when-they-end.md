# Group Periods close as soon as they end; Seasons keep a 72-hour grace

A Group's weekly, monthly and yearly results become final the moment the Period has ended in every member's time zone, and the winner's crown moment shows on their next app open. We dropped the 72-hour grace for late syncs that we first planned, because a crown that arrives days into the next Period has lost its moment. The accepted cost: iPhones can't sync in the background, so a Session logged with no signal syncs only when the app is next opened online, and if that happens after its Period has closed, the Session still counts toward the user's Points, Rank and Streak but not toward that Period's board. Seasons keep the 72-hour grace before their final Rank is frozen, because no celebration waits on it (a Rank-up shows as soon as it happens) and it lets a Session from the last day still count toward the Rank that sets the next Season's starting points.

## Considered Options

- **A 72-hour grace for Periods too**: every late sync counts, but a weekly crown lands on Thursday, after the next week has started.
- **Crown at the end, board open for 72 hours**: the crown is immediate, but a late sync could hand the win to someone else after the first winner has celebrated.
