# Sex and bodyweight are collected so they can be cancelled out

The pitch is that anyone can compete with their friends regardless of sex or body size, yet the app asks for both. They determine each lifter's Strength Level, which weights Progress Points so that a small gain by an advanced lifter is worth as much as a large gain by a beginner; the app can only make sex and bodyweight not matter if it knows them. Both stay private and are never shown to Group members, and "prefer not to say" uses the midpoint between the male and female standards.

## Considered Options

- **Self-reported years of training**: needs no body data, but anyone can claim ten years to inflate their points, and years don't distinguish a serious lifter from a casual one.
- **Time since joining the app**: treats experienced lifters who join as beginners, so they lose for months.
