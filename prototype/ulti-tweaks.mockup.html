Day colours rotate pink, cyan, lime, coral, violet and stay with a Day when it's dragged. Folded, a Day shows its name and exercise count. Opened, the rows and their remove buttons work as they do today (left out here for clarity).

Choices in these mockups that go beyond what we agreed

  1. History rows reuse the Routine Day's colour as a dot (screens 1–3), tying history to the Routine.
  2. Editing a past Set moves into a sheet opened by tapping the Set (screen 3), instead of Edit and Delete links on every line.
  3. Group settings lists the members again (screen 5), because the Owner needs somewhere to remove them.
  4. The current week shows in History too, with a dashed stamp until it's earned (screen 2).
